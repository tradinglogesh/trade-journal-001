Trade Journal - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

What the app does

Trade Journal is a personal trading journal. Your data is stored in your browser and synced to your own account in the cloud (Supabase).

Information we store

  • Account: your email address and name, used to sign you in and keep each account's data separate.
  • Journal data: trades, notes, plans and chart images. Row Level Security ensures only you can read or change your records.

Third parties

Data is sent only between your browser and Supabase (authentication, database and storage). No Google services, analytics or advertising are used. Your data is never sold or shared.

Deleting your data

Download a JSON backup from Settings, then contact the address below to have your account and all its data deleted.
