Trade Journal - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

What the app does

Trade Journal is a personal trading journal that runs entirely in your browser. There is no app server and no database operated by the developer.

Information we access

  • Google profile (name, email, picture): used only to show who is signed in and to keep each account's data separate.
  • Google Drive (drive.file scope): the app can only see and modify the single backup file it creates (trade-journal-backup.json). It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

How your data is used and stored

Your journal data (trades, notes, plans, images) is stored in your browser's local storage and in the backup file in your own Google Drive, so it can sync across your devices. The developer cannot access your data and does not collect, sell, share, or analyse it.

Third parties

Data is sent only between your browser and Google (sign-in and Drive). No analytics or advertising services are used.

Deleting your data

Sign out and clear the site data in your browser, delete trade-journal-backup.json from your Google Drive, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
